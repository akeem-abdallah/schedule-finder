A

Ink & paper

Biggest departure. Serif headings are a real commitment — distinctive, but a serif on a dense timetable is a taste call you'd be stuck with.

B

Structural

Strongest personality and the best-functioning grid. Risk: the hard black rules and uppercase can feel severe if you wanted friendly.

C

Warm stone

Safest. Keeps everything you already approved and only swaps the palette off the shelf. Least distinctive of the three, but nothing about it reads as generated.

Mixing is fine and probably right — e.g. B's grid treatment with C's warm neutrals, or A's palette with a sans heading instead of the serif. Say what you like from each.
